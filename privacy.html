Privacy Policy

Minimal data collection for digital delivery.

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Information collected

When a buyer purchases through Lemon Squeezy, payment, tax, receipt, and checkout information is handled by Lemon Squeezy. This site may ask for a Hugging Face username so private repository access can be granted after purchase.

How information is used

Buyer information is used to deliver the purchased dataset, grant private repository access, provide support, prevent fraud or abuse, and maintain purchase records.

What is not collected

The datasets sold by this business are synthetic. They are not built from buyer customer lists, patient files, private records, or other real personal datasets submitted through this website.

Optional website analytics

When website analytics is enabled and you choose Allow analytics, Google Analytics measures page visits and clicks on dataset previews, examples, and commercial access links. Page URLs and referrers are reduced to approved page and campaign information before measurement. Buyer usernames, email addresses, payment details, form values, and full checkout URLs are excluded from these events.

Google may process technical information such as browser and device details, approximate location derived from the connection, and analytics cookie identifiers. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled in this site's analytics code. A click toward checkout is measured separately from a completed sale; payment and receipt handling remain with Lemon Squeezy.

You can decline analytics and still inspect datasets or purchase. Analytics preferences are saved in this browser's local storage. When analytics is enabled, use Analytics preferences in the footer to change your choice. Choosing No thanks stops further analytics events from this site and clears its scoped Google Analytics cookies. See Google's privacy policy for information about Google's processing.
