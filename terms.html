Product and delivery

HaseebDev Synthetic Data sells premade digital synthetic dataset packages through Lemon Squeezy. Delivery is provided by granting access to a private Hugging Face repository or another agreed digital delivery location.

License

After purchase, the buyer receives a commercial-use license for the delivered dataset package for internal AI training, evaluation, testing, prototyping, and workflow development. The buyer may not resell, redistribute, publicly mirror, or repackage the dataset as a standalone competing dataset product.

Synthetic data notice

The datasets are synthetic and are not represented as real customer, patient, financial, legal, medical, or government records. Buyers are responsible for validating that a dataset is suitable for their intended use and regulatory environment.

Access requirements

Buyers must provide a valid Hugging Face username so repository access can be granted. Access may be delayed if the username is missing, invalid, or cannot receive access to the private repository.

Restrictions

  • No illegal, harmful, deceptive, or privacy-invasive use.
  • No claim that synthetic records are real-world records.
  • No resale or redistribution as a standalone dataset library.
  • No attempt to identify real people from synthetic examples.
